## The problem
Property investing involves more than tracking a lead. Investors need to keep opportunities, contacts, follow-ups and portfolio obligations connected as a deal moves through its lifecycle.

## My contribution
I co-founded PropertyPipeline and led its technical architecture, product development, integrations and operational infrastructure. The work combines direct knowledge of the customer workflow with the practical demands of running a subscription product.

## The approach
The platform uses tenant-specific deployments, automation and integrations to support a specialist CRM workflow. I later added a read-only MCP interface so an authenticated user can explore their own data through compatible AI clients, while keeping access tied to their account.

## The result
PropertyPipeline is an operating SaaS product used by property investors. It remains an ongoing exercise in turning domain-specific requests into reliable features and maintaining the system that delivers them.
